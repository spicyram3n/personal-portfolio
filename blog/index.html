Everything here is written as a series: one system, taken apart chapter by chapter, with the maths set beside the code that implements it. These are notes from my MSc work as it happens, not tutorials written afterwards.

Series 01 — Building Structure from Motion from scratch

Four chapters · complete · Python, NumPy, OpenCV

A ground-up implementation of two-view Structure from Motion, from raw image pairs to a coloured 3D point cloud. Hand-rolled 8-point algorithm, RANSAC, DLT triangulation and a full evaluation suite. Code: github.com/spicyram3n/casualSfM.

Chapter 01 · February 2026 Feature detection & matching SIFT keypoints, the 128-dimensional descriptor, and Lowe's ratio test. Chapter 02 · March 2026 The fundamental matrix The epipolar constraint, Hartley normalisation, SVD, and the symmetric epipolar distance inside RANSAC. Chapter 03 · April 2026 Pose recovery & triangulation The essential matrix, the cheirality check, DLT triangulation, and assembling the pipeline. Chapter 04 · April 2026 Evaluation & visualisation 701 points at 0.50 px mean reprojection error, the depth distribution, and inspecting the result in Rerun.
