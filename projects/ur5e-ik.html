UR5e IK Control in MuJoCo

Robotics · 2024 · personal project
MuJoCo · Python · SciPy

A modular inverse-kinematics framework for the UR5e, built so that the solver is a swappable component and the comparison is like-for-like. Three are implemented: gradient descent, SLSQP, and damped least squares.

They are benchmarked across both comfortably reachable targets and near-singular configurations, which is where the differences actually show up — damping earns its keep exactly when the Jacobian stops being well conditioned and the undamped solvers start asking for enormous joint velocities.
