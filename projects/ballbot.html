BallBot

Robotics · 2024 · personal project
ROS 2 · Gazebo · OpenCV · Python

A small differential-drive robot that detects a coloured ball and drives at it. Detection is real-time HSV segmentation, which is the right tool here: the target is defined by its colour, and thresholding in HSV survives the lighting changes that wreck the same trick in RGB.

The offset between the ball's centroid and the image centre feeds a PID velocity controller whose output is published straight to ROS 2 velocity topics. The whole loop runs in simulation in Gazebo.
