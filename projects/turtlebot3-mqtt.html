MQTT Remote Control of TurtleBot3

Robotics · 2024 · personal project
ROS 2 · Gazebo · MQTT

ROS 2's own transport is not built to cross the open internet. This bridges it: an MQTT broker sits in the middle, and small bridge nodes on either side translate between MQTT topics and ROS 2 publishers and subscribers.

Teleoperation commands go out from one machine and telemetry comes back, with sub-80 ms observed latency over the public internet — low enough to drive by hand without fighting the delay.
